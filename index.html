About Me

A Story Told
Through the Lens

I'm Alex Morgan, a professional photographer based in New York City. My passion for capturing the world began in the misty Scottish Highlands, where I first picked up a camera at age 16. Since then, photography has become my life's work and greatest love.

I specialize in creating timeless imagery that speaks to the soul — whether it's an intimate portrait session, a breathtaking landscape, or the joyful chaos of a wedding day. Every frame is crafted with intention, patience, and a deep respect for the moment.
